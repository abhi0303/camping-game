A world that never ends and is saved online. Up to 4 members; any of them can open it, alone or together — it remembers everything when nobody is playing. No zombies here.

Survival rules
  • Nights fall after 3, 6, then every 10 min — zombies get tougher at every checkpoint
  • Go down and nobody revives you? You're out — you watch your team
  • The RV only drives at 100% — repair it with the hammer
  • Build towers at Watchtower Valley to survive the siege
  • Then a stone castle against 10 giants at the Giant Plateau
  • Most zombie kills + revives wins the scoreboard
